## Decision contract and scientific boundary

HeadroomDecision has strict schema/version and required producer/base/updater/source manifest/split/subject-set/teacher hashes; candidate/oracle scopes, seeds, no-op/random/oracle gains, paired effects and uncertainty, decision, reviewer, creation time and evidence artifact identities. Finite values, unique subjects, exact winner identity/confirmation and source compatibility are validated. Base/updater checkpoint byte hashes and common base/D component lineage must be checked, not trusted from filenames. Development subject-set identity is bound to the decision; S2 subjects are separately producer-fit and must share the reviewed split/role allocation, not be forced to equal development subjects.

The initial four-subject NEXT-1 is explicitly early and **INCONCLUSIVE for final transition**, regardless of apparent positive gains. Acceptance requires human/scientific review, a positive practical margin frozen before evaluation (and a positive predeclared equivalence margin when claiming equivalence), and adequately precise paired subject evidence (independent-subject floor32 that cannot be lowered to open MAIN; not a power guarantee). Classification distinguishes confirmed Oracle>Z0 and Oracle>Random, useful correction with no selection need, no observed headroom with adequate precision, and inconclusive uncertainty. No learned-route claim is part of NEXT-1. A string `HEADROOM_CONFIRMED` or reviewer name alone cannot bypass evidence/provenance validation; MAIN banking must validate the bound reviewed evidence and all identities. Dirty source or engineering/synthetic evidence cannot authorize MAIN.

The CLI NEXT-1 recipe remains locked to four subjects. The lower-level diagnostic/evidence options may describe an explicitly sized later development cohort of at least four subjects; retained `max_subjects`, actual subject count and cohort identities must agree. MAIN admission checks the existing role manifest's related-group assignments and rejects repeated groups in the reviewed development cohort; distinct subject IDs alone cannot certify independence. This permits a coherent future reviewed cohort artifact and a meaningful positive admission unit fixture without pretending that a four-subject execution produced 32 independent subjects. No larger cohort is executed in this patch.

### Required R4B composition (review clarification)

Do not compose separate end-to-end random/oracle runs and infer equality afterward. For each subject: create exactly one frozen observation context/Z0; generate one typed complete proposal batch; filter support-legal rows and freeze the sampled candidate pool; choose all three random rows from that same pool before any target callback; use existing `apply_scored_action`/decoder for target-free controls; join target once; use existing teacher primitives for fixed-Q screening and an independent exact-footprint measurement of the same selected stored action, including nonpositive screening winners; decode the actual oracle candidate and any no-op choice distinctly. Save subject-keyed real records, not positional list joins or missing-metric zero fallbacks. Delta hashes come from delta tensors/action identities, not hashes of measured labels. Writer is additive/unclipped; saturation is not applicable unless a specifically defined bound-proximity statistic is separately measured. The final validator recomputes paired subject effects/uncertainty from bound complete records and checks every subject's winner/confirmation identity. A self-declared precise flag and a hash of an arbitrary summary are insufficient. Both Oracle-minus-Z0 and Oracle-minus-Random confidence lower bounds must exceed the predeclared practical margin; review JSON must bind the retained evidence, options and identities. An ignored/untracked executable file is included in the live manifest but cannot receive CLEAN/reconstructable provenance merely because Git status hides it.
