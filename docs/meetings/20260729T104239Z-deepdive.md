## Health: NO ACTIVE RUN

The current branch has a coherent four-phase research specification but no
executable package, tests, dependency manifest, data-legality boundary, or
physical observation operator. Only `docs/` is authoritative; deprecated root
documents are excluded from decisions.

## External Signals (Researcher)

- GaussianPile (CVPR 2026), GSVR, GaussianSVR, and MedGS directly collide with
  broad novelty claims around slice-aware Gaussian medical reconstruction.
- GSDF, SurfaceSplat, GaussianUDF, Scaffold-GS, and 3D-SLNR collide with generic
  SDF-Gaussian, anchor, and shared-local-field claims.
- GauSS-MI and ActiveGAMER make generic uncertainty or information-gain
  routing insufficient as a contribution.
- Imaging-physics-aware plane/slab operators and explicit PSF treatment are
  baseline expectations for slice-volume work.
- The defensible thesis is leakage-safe, calibrated active multi-sequence plane
  selection over a validated PSF-aware Gaussian state.
- The initial task must be described as retrospective budgeted progressive
  observation unless a real scanner-side plane/stack protocol is supplied.

## Internal Health (Architect)

### Blockers

- No enforceable sparse-manifest, commit/reveal, or isolated audit boundary.
- No canonical RAS-mm, tensor-axis, source-affine, or signed-normal contract.
- No differentiable physical-plane/slab renderer or independent numeric oracle.
- No package, dependency declaration, test suite, or reproducibility substrate.

### High severity

- The documented encoder gate depends on a downstream renderer that does not
  exist, so encoder-first results would not validate the locked architecture.
- SDF behavior, uncertainty semantics, covariance parameterization, cache
  lifetime, and routing supervision remain experimentally unresolved.
- The paper scope contains too many simultaneous contributions for causal
  attribution.

### Dependency decision

- Start with a CPU-first PyTorch reference and dependency-light contracts.
- Defer MONAI, graph libraries, `gsplat`, custom CUDA, and medical I/O adapters
  until the reference kernel and legality gates pass.
